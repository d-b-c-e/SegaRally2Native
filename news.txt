# Sega Rally 2 Native: what is new, for the players.
#
# The game reads this file from GitHub (main branch) at launch. When it has an
# entry with a higher number than the news.txt in the player's folder, the main
# menu shows those entries in an UPDATE AVAILABLE window with UPDATE NOW
# (update.bat) and LATER. Commits that do not add an entry show nothing, so
# several fixes can go out together: add the entry when they are all in.
#
# Format:
#   [N] title      starts entry N (a number one higher than the last; the
#                  title is usually the date)
#   - text         a line of the entry (lines starting "- " get a bullet)
#   # ...          a comment, never shown
# Newest entry at the top. Keep each one short: about ten lines fit.

[6] 12 November 2026
- Attract demos: the cars now sit on the road instead of hovering over it, and keep their windows in place.
- Smooth motion in the attract demos and replays: the wheels no longer show through the bodywork of a car coming fast at the camera.

[5] 12 November 2026
- Closer to the arcade: the cars' white paint no longer looks grey, with or without ray tracing, and sunny stages are no longer darkened.
- BUY ME A COFFEE has moved to MORE RECOMPS.

[4] 11 November 2026
- Shadows: without ray tracing the cars have the arcade's shadow underneath again, so they no longer seem to float.
- Without ray tracing, the sun's shadows have smooth edges instead of blocky steps.

[3] 11 November 2026
- No more console window behind the game.
- Building: fixed a Visual Studio setup error on PCs with an unusual PATH, and the NVIDIA DLSS download is now optional.

[2] 11 November 2026
- Engine sound fixed: the engines play through the sound chip's effects processor, which a bug in the emulation kept silent. Thanks to the Reddit player who noticed!

[1] 11 November 2026
- 14 achievements, from your first finish to champion of the championship.
- RECORDS is back in the main menu, with ACHIEVEMENTS and STATS tabs: races, championships, best laps and times on each course.
- MORE RECOMPS now lists Star Wars Trilogy too.
